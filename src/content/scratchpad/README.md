## 💼 Work

_Focuses on weeknotes, public sector transformation, learning design, and consulting._

### 1. Weeknotes: First Impressions at LAA

- **Angle**: Reflect on your first few weeks working with the LAA Digital team.
- **Key Themes**: Agile practices in justice system digital service design, mapping stakeholders, and starting new projects.
- **Format**: Traditional weeknote structure.

### 2. Designing the Curriculum: Building a Consulting Skills Program

- **Angle**: Connect your Master's studies in Creative Teaching and Learning Design with your day job at TPXimpact.
- **Key Themes**: How you are designing the program, learning theories applied (e.g., experiential learning, case-based learning), and challenges in upskilling modern consultants.

### 3. Psychological Safety in Learning Environments: From Research to Practice

- **Angle**: Introduce your current university project researching psychological safety in educational contexts, sharing what the research has shown you and the resource you are developing for other learning designers.

- **Format**: Academic reflection / design log.

## 🛋️ Rest

_Focuses on life transitions, mindfulness, fitness recovery, books, and self-care._

### 5. The Comfort Watch: Quiet Media for Anxious Days

- **Angle**: Reflecting on the power of "comfort viewing"—choosing simple, familiar, and heartwarming films (like _The Secret Life of Walter Mitty_ or _Forrest Gump_) to quiet the mind during periods of high anxiety or exhaustion, rather than feeling pressured to watch "challenging" or "highbrow" TV.
- **Key Themes**: Embracing distraction as a form of self-care; the comfort of predictable narratives; identifying the movies that act as your personal security blankets.
- **Format**: Personal essay / media reflection.

### 6. The Art of Rebuilding: Navigating Life's Major Transitions

- **Angle**: Sharing a personal perspective on navigating divorce, mediation, and finding a new sense of equilibrium.
- **Key Themes**: Balancing study/work, allocating time for social life, and cultivating routines that protect your mental health.
- **Format**: Personal essay / digital garden reflection.

### 7. June Playlist: Sounds for Rest and Recovery

- **Angle**: Share a curated musical playlist for the month of June, writing a short piece on what music has been helping you relax, study, and recharge.
- **Key Themes**: Music as a tool for rest and mindfulness; introducing your new [PlaylistWidget](file:///Users/d1sc0/Projects/hellostu--xyz/src/components/for_posts/PlaylistWidget/PlaylistWidget.astro) to readers; the tracks and artists that defined your month.
- **Format**: Music post / curation.

### 8. Limbo: A Mid-Year Life Update

- **Angle**: A candid, personal life update reflecting on being in a state of transition and uncertainty across several fronts.
- **Key Themes**: Navigating the waiting game and limbo on the housing front; the adjustment of returning to full-time work; reflections on being "unlucky in love" and finding peace in self-reliance and small daily comforts.
- **Format**: Personal update / weeknote.

## 🎸 Play

_Focuses on creative hobbies, photography, music, and tech projects._

### 9. Harry Styles Live in Amsterdam: The Power of Shared Joy

- **Angle**: Reflecting on your trip to Amsterdam to see Harry Styles live, focusing on how experiencing the concert with close friends who are huge fans made their excitement catching, and exploring a personal reflection on finding deep happiness in making others happy and sharing in their joy.
- **Key Themes**: The contagious nature of excitement when shared with close, passionate friends; reflections on the personal fulfillment that comes from facilitating and witnessing others' happiness; the warmth of close personal connections vs. anonymous crowds.
- **Format**: Personal reflection / travel essay.

### 10. Finding a New Rhythm: A Drummer’s Progress Report

- **Angle**: Document your journey learning to play the drums.
- **Key Themes**: Hand-eye coordination, the meditative state of focusing purely on rhythm, and your favorite tracks to practice on your kit.
- **Format**: Hobby journal.

### 11. Under the Hood: Rebuilding my Digital Garden with Astro v6

- **Angle**: Share the technical story of migrating this website, setting up Sveltia CMS, and adding the AI recommendations engine.
- **Key Themes**: The Astro Content Layer API, integrating Puppeteer for automated OG image generation, and using Gemini embeddings to calculate post similarity.
- **Format**: Tech blog post (highly engaging for other developers!).

### 12. Pair Programming with Antigravity: My First Agentic AI Writing Partner

- **Angle**: Reflecting on the experience of co-authoring and building this blog with an agentic AI coder (Antigravity). Discussing the shift from treating AI as an autocomplete search bar to treating it as an active collaborator with access to your workspace and tools.
- **Key Themes**: The mechanics of human-agent pair programming; how agentic tools speed up chores (like folder structure adjustments and config updates); using AI as a cognitive sounding board for mapping and sorting thoughts.
- **Format**: Tech/philosophical essay.
